## 🚀 Overview

A luxury client-facing single-page editorial presentation for **DONGFENG COMMERCIAL EGYPT**. 
Features a continuous vertical scroll, dual-language engine (English / Arabic with native RTL/LTR layout), and 24 strategic marketing chapters.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Bilingual Dual Engine**: Instant switching between English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) with authentic typography (Noto Sans Arabic, Cairo, Plus Jakarta Sans, Space Grotesk).
- **24 Art-Directed Chapters**: Portfolio ecosystems, customer problem contrasts, brand positioning, audience segmentation, 8 signature series, 90-day execution roadmap, and KPI dashboards.
- **Dedicated 9:16 Vertical Video Player**: Embedded official vertical commercial campaign format with custom playback controls.
- **Interactive B2B Tools**:
  - *Interactive Lead Qualification Simulator* (Chapter 13)
  - *Find Your Dongfeng Business Selector* (Chapter 14)
  - *Social Media Mix Distribution Engine* (Chapter 10)
  - *Full Funnel & Retargeting Automation Map* (Chapters 12 & 21)
  - *90-Day Interactive Sprint Planner* (Chapter 22)
- **Local Brand Assets**: Official Dongfeng Commercial logos, ProMedia branding, and leadership photography.
